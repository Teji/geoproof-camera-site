GeoProof Camera Privacy Policy

Effective date: 21 September 2026

GeoProof Camera is a location-stamped photo and video application designed for field-work records. This policy explains what information the app processes, where it is stored, and when limited data leaves the device.

1. No account, advertising or analytics

GeoProof Camera does not require an account. The app does not include advertising services or analytics SDKs. The developer does not operate a server that receives your photos, videos, employee name, staff ID or organisation details.

2. Information stored on your device

Employee details, organisation details and the GPS-verified work location are stored locally in Android app storage. Captured photos and videos are saved in the GeoProof folder in your device gallery. These items remain under your control and are shared only when you use Android's Share function.

3. Location and route calculation

Precise foreground location is used to create proof stamps, display coordinates and accuracy, save a GPS-verified work location, and calculate distance from that work location. The app does not request background location access.

To calculate driving distance and estimated travel time, the current coordinates and saved work-location coordinates are transmitted over an encrypted HTTPS connection to the public OSRM routing service. Photos, videos, employee details and staff IDs are not included in that request. The routing provider may process technical logs according to its own policies.

4. Camera, microphone and sensors

Camera access is used to capture photos and videos. Microphone access is used only to record video audio. The device compass or rotation sensor is used to display direction. Camera media and sensor readings are processed on the device, except for the limited route-coordinate request described above.

5. Voluntary project support

If you choose “Buy me a coffee,” the app opens an external Buy Me a Coffee page in your browser. GeoProof Camera does not process or store payment information. Buy Me a Coffee's own terms and privacy policy apply to transactions completed on its website. Supporting the project is optional and does not unlock any app feature, content, badge, reward or special access.

6. Retention and deletion

You can delete captured media through the app gallery or your device gallery. You can remove locally stored profile and work-location settings by clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app. The developer cannot delete third-party routing logs because the developer does not control the public routing service.

7. Permissions and choices

You can revoke camera, microphone or location permission through Android Settings. Core proof-capture features may not work without camera and precise location access.

8. Children

GeoProof Camera is a general field-work utility and is not directed to children. The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

9. Security and accuracy

Route requests use HTTPS encryption in transit. GPS accuracy, reverse-geocoded addresses and route estimates can vary because of device conditions and third-party service availability.

10. Policy changes

This policy may be updated when app functionality, service providers or legal requirements change. The effective date at the top of this page will be revised when changes are published.
