Privacy policy

Clear information about your data.

GeoProof Camera is designed for field-work records. This policy explains what the Android app processes, what stays on your device and when limited data leaves it.

On this page Overview Device storage Location & routing Permissions Project support Retention Security Contact
Effective date: 21 September 2026 Applies to: GeoProof Camera for Android

1. No account, advertising or analytics

GeoProof Camera does not require an account. The app does not include advertising services or analytics SDKs. The developer does not operate a server that receives your photos, videos, employee name, staff ID or organisation details.

In short: no sign-in, no advertising profile and no developer analytics dashboard.

2. Information stored on your device

Employee details, organisation details and the GPS-verified work location are stored locally in Android app storage. Captured photos and videos are saved in the GeoProof folder in your device gallery. These items remain under your control and are shared only when you use Android’s Share function.

3. Location and route calculation

Precise foreground location is used to create proof stamps, display coordinates and accuracy, save a GPS-verified work location, and calculate distance from that work location. Latitude and longitude for the work location come from the phone’s GPS and are not manually editable. The app does not request background location access.

To calculate driving distance and estimated travel time, the current coordinates and saved work-location coordinates are transmitted over an encrypted HTTPS connection to the public OSRM routing service. Photos, videos, employee details and staff IDs are not included in that request. The routing provider may process technical logs according to its own policies.

4. Camera, microphone, location and sensors

Camera access is used to capture photos and videos. Microphone access is used only to record video audio. Precise location is used for capture details, work-location setup and routing. The device compass or rotation sensor is used to display direction. Camera media and sensor readings are processed on the device, except for the limited route-coordinate request described above.

You can revoke camera, microphone or location permission through Android Settings. Core proof-capture features may not work without camera and precise location access.

5. Voluntary project support

If you choose “Buy me a coffee,” the app or this website opens an external Buy Me a Coffee page in your browser. GeoProof Camera does not process or store payment information. Buy Me a Coffee’s own terms and privacy policy apply to transactions completed on its website.

Supporting the project is optional and does not unlock any app feature, content, badge, reward or special access.

6. Retention and deletion

You can delete captured media through the app gallery or your device gallery. You can remove locally stored profile and work-location settings by clearing the app’s data or uninstalling the app. The developer cannot delete third-party routing logs because the developer does not control the public routing service.

7. Children

GeoProof Camera is a general field-work utility and is not directed to children. The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Security, accuracy and limitations

Route requests use HTTPS encryption in transit. GPS accuracy, reverse-geocoded addresses, compass readings and route estimates can vary because of device conditions and third-party service availability. Users should follow their organisation’s verification, safety and legal requirements.

9. Policy changes

This policy may be updated when app functionality, service providers or legal requirements change. The effective date at the top of this page will be revised when changes are published.

10. Contact

For privacy or support questions, use the developer contact details published on GeoProof Camera’s Google Play listing. Until the listing is live, you can visit the project support page.
