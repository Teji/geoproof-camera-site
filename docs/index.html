Questions

Good to know before you capture.

Simple answers about storage, location and sharing.

Does the app upload my photos or videos?

No. Captured media is saved to the GeoProof folder in your device gallery and is shared only when you choose to share it.

How is the work location saved?

The app uses the phone’s live GPS location. Latitude and longitude are not manually editable, helping prevent accidental or arbitrary work coordinates.

Is “distance from work” a straight line?

No. GeoProof requests a driving route estimate from the public OSRM service and displays road distance with estimated travel time.

Does video include the same proof stamp?

Yes. Photos and videos include the same core location, direction, route, identity, time and Proof ID information.

Does the app run location tracking in the background?

No. The app uses foreground location for setup and capture features; it does not request background location permission.

Is project support required?

No. Supporting the project is entirely optional and does not unlock features, rewards or special access.
